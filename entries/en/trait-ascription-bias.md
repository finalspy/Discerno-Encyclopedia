## How it works

Kammer (1982) observes that people perceive more variability in their own traits than in those of a friend.

## Why it is problematic

Others are reduced to a stable label, and the expectations one has of them can feed a snap judgment.

## How to detect it

- "He's always like that", based on a few situations.
- One describes oneself with nuances, and the other without any.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for situations in which the person acted differently.
- Describe what they did, not what they would be.
