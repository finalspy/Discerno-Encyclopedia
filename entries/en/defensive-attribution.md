## How it works

Shaver (1970) observes that the responsibility assigned depends on the severity of the outcome and on our closeness to the situation. The direction of the effect varies depending on whether we identify with the actor or with the victim.

## Why it is problematic

The judgment of responsibility then depends on our need for reassurance, rather than on the facts.

## How to detect it

- The more serious the outcome, the heavier the fault appears, for identical conduct.
- One distinguishes oneself from the person affected right away.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate the conduct without regard to its outcome.
- Ask yourself with whom you identify in this story.
