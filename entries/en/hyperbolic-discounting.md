## How it works

Ainslie (1975) describes a preference that reverses as the deadline approaches. Laibson (1997) integrates it into an economic model of saving. One may prefer to wait for a distant gain, then give in when it becomes near.

## Why it is problematic

Installment payments, instant credit and instant rewards push the cost to later, when less attention is paid to it.

## How to detect it

- The benefit is immediate and the cost comes later.
- The monthly payment is emphasized, rather than the total.

## How to protect yourself

- Calculate the total cost over the full term.
- Decide in advance, and automate what matters in the long term.
