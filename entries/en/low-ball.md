## How it works

Cialdini and colleagues (1978) show that a person already committed often maintains the decision when the real cost is revealed later, even though they would have refused had they known it from the start.

## Why it is problematic

Commitment serves as a lever: one has the impression of deciding freely while the decisive information has been withheld.

## How to detect it

- Fees, conditions or constraints that appear at the final step.
- The displayed price differs from the final price.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for the total cost before committing.
- Allow yourself to reverse a decision when the conditions change.
