## How it works

Roediger and Karpicke (2006) compare rereading and recall: over the long term, those who tested themselves retain more.

## Why it is problematic

This is not a flaw: it is a useful result for learning. It has a downside, because rereading gives a misleading feeling of mastery.

## How to detect it

- One feels comfortable with a text that has been reread, without being able to explain it.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask yourself questions rather than rereading.
- Use these entries to quiz yourself on a real example.
