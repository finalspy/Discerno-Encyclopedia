## How it works

"Natural" serves as proof in place of an evaluation. Yet natural substances can be dangerous, and synthetic products can be safe. Moore (1903) criticized a similar line of reasoning in ethics: the idea of deducing what is good from what is natural.

## Why it is problematic

The argument avoids examining actual effects and serves as a commercial or ideological label.

## How to detect it

- "Natural" or "chemical" stands in for an argument.
- No trial or safety data is given.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for proof of the effect and of the risk, whatever the origin.
- Remember that "natural" often has no precise definition.
