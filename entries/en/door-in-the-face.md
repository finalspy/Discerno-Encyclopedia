## How it works

Cialdini and colleagues (1975) obtain more agreement to a small request when it follows the refusal of a very large one. The retreat is perceived as a concession, which calls for a concession in return, and the second request seems reasonable by contrast.

## Why it is problematic

The point of comparison is manufactured: the final request can remain higher than one would have accepted from the outset.

## How to detect it

- An outsized request is followed by a quick retreat.
- "Well, then at least…".

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate the second request on its own merits.
- Ask whether you would have accepted it if it had been made alone.
