## Definition

Hofstadter, a cognitive scientist, formulated the law in Hofstadter (1979), in connection with forecasts about chess programs. The law cites itself: knowing that one underestimates delays is not enough to correct the estimate.

Status: a humorous saying, which echoes a studied bias, the planning fallacy.

## Competing definitions and debates

The underlying phenomenon is documented by research on the planning fallacy, which has its own entry. The recursive form of the law is a witticism, not a measure of the gap.

## Why it helps to discern

A time promise, one's own or someone else's, is better compared with the actual duration of similar projects than with the ideal scenario.
