## How it works

The norm of reciprocity (Gouldner, 1960) pushes people to return what they have received. Regan (1971) observes that participants who were offered a drink later bought more raffle tickets from the person who had offered it.

## Why it is problematic

The gesture is not free: it creates a debt that one did not choose, sometimes far heavier than the gift.

## How to detect it

- An unsolicited gift precedes a request.
- A concession is staged in order to claim one in return.

## How to protect yourself

- Separate the gift from the decision.
- Accept without committing oneself, or decline the gift when in doubt.
