## How it works

To describe the same fact, one can choose a neutral word or a word that already passes judgment. The word conveys an evaluation without justifying it. The reader can adopt it without noticing that it has been offered.

## Why it is problematic

The conclusion slips into the vocabulary, which avoids having to defend it.

## How to detect it

- Replacing a word with its neutral equivalent changes the overall impression.
- Adjectives and metaphors carry the judgment, not the facts.
- One side receives flattering words, the other derogatory words.

## How to protect yourself

- Rewrite the sentence in neutral terms and see what remains.
- Apply the reversal test to the other side.
