## How it works

"I know my father. I do not know the masked man. Therefore the masked man is not my father." What one knows about a thing is not enough to define what it is: our knowledge may be incomplete.

## Why it is problematic

Confusing what is known with what is true leads to reasoning of the type: "No one has told me that X is Y, so it is not Y."

## How to detect it

- A conclusion about reality rests on what someone knows or believes.
- The same object is designated in two ways.

## How to protect yourself

- Distinguish what is true from what a person knows about it.
- Check whether two designations refer to the same thing.
