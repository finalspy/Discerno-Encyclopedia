## How it works

Brenner (1973) observes that a person waiting for their turn to speak poorly remembers what came just before and what they themselves say. Attention is taken up by preparation.

## Why it is problematic

In a debate, one may fail to hear what the other has just said, and respond beside the point, in good faith.

## How to detect it

- A reply does not take into account what has just been said.

## How to protect yourself

- Take notes while listening.
- Restate what the other person said before responding.
