## How it works

Anderson, Bjork and Bjork (1994) show that reviewing part of a category weakens the later recall of its other elements.

## Why it is problematic

Repeating only some facts can make others be forgotten, including those that qualify the picture.

## How to detect it

- A narrative always repeats the same facts.
- Items that were once known seem to disappear from the discussion.

## How to protect yourself

- Go back to the complete sources.
- Look for what the usual account leaves out.
