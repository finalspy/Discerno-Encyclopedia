## How it works

Loewenstein, O'Donoghue and Rabin (2003) model this tendency: we project our present preferences into the future, which explains, for example, purchases made on an empty stomach.

## Why it is problematic

Subscriptions and long-term commitments are signed under the influence of the desire of the moment.

## How to detect it

- A long-term commitment is offered at the moment when the desire is strongest.
- "You will always want this."

## How to protect yourself

- Wait until returning to a calmer state.
- Choose commitments that are easy to exit.
