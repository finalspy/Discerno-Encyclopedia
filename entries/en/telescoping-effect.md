## How it works

Neter and Waksberg (1964) describe this shift in surveys that ask households about their recent spending. Events from outside the period are wrongly reported as falling within it.

## Why it is problematic

Surveys based on memory can overestimate recent events and give a false impression of a trend.

## How to detect it

- "It was barely a while ago..." with no verified date.
- A survey relies on dated recollections with no verification.

## How to protect yourself

- Check dates against archives.
- Look at how the survey bounds the period.
