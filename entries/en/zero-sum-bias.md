## How it works

Meegan (2010) observes that students perceive competition even when good grades are not limited. Różycka-Tran and colleagues (2015) compare this belief across 37 countries.

## Why it is problematic

Some situations are genuinely zero-sum, others are not. Presenting a subject as zero-sum can set groups against each other when a shared gain is possible.

## How to detect it

- "If they gain, we lose", without figures.
- No common option is considered.

## How to protect yourself

- Check whether the resource is really limited.
- Look for options that benefit both parties.
