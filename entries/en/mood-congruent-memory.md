## How it works

Bower (1981) shows that an induced mood favors the recall of memories of the same tone. The effect has been qualified depending on the protocols.

## Why it is problematic

A bad mood brings up bad memories, and gives the feeling that everything is going wrong. Anxiety-inducing content can sustain this cycle.

## How to detect it

- Everything seems negative after an alarming piece of news.
- The overall judgment changes with mood.

## How to protect yourself

- Postpone important judgments.
- Look for written or numerical evidence.
