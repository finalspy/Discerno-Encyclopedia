## How it works

Tversky and Kahneman (1981) show that an option seems safe when its outcome is presented as guaranteed at one stage, even if that stage is reached only with a certain probability.

## Why it is problematic

A partial guarantee can seem total, like insurance whose limits are not specified.

## How to detect it

- "Guaranteed", followed by barely visible conditions.
- An overall risk is broken down into stages.

## How to protect yourself

- Calculate the overall probability.
- Read the conditions of the guarantee.
