## Definition

The implicit association test of Greenwald, McGhee and Schwartz (1998) compares response times for pairing concepts with evaluations.

## Competing definitions and debates

Its meaning is debated. Oswald and colleagues (2013) conclude that this test poorly predicts individual discriminatory behavior, whereas other researchers defend its value at the level of groups. The test does not constitute a diagnosis of a person.

## Why it helps to discern

Knowing this debate avoids two errors: presenting an individual score as proof of a prejudice, or concluding that automatic associations do not exist.
