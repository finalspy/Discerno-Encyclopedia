## Definition

Greenwald and Banaji (1995) propose that past experiences influence judgments without being identified as such. Stereotypes are part of them.

## Competing definitions and debates

The share played by the unconscious, the stability of these measures and their link with behavior are debated. The notion does not allow a conclusion about any specific person.

## Why it helps to discern

The notion invites checking decisions against explicit criteria and data, rather than attributing hidden thoughts to people.
