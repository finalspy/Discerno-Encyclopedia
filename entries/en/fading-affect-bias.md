## How it works

Walker, Skowronski and Thompson (2003) synthesize work relying in particular on personal diaries: the intensity of negative events generally fades faster.

## Why it is problematic

This tendency helps us live, but it can also make us forget the real hardships of a past period.

## How to detect it

- The past looks gentler than what the records of the time say.

## How to protect yourself

- Reread what you wrote at the time before comparing.
