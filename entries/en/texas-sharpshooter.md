## How it works

The image is that of a marksman who paints the target around his bullet holes. Thompson (2009) describes this risk when criteria are set after the result has been seen. Ioannidis (2005) shows more broadly that analytical flexibility undermines published results.

## Why it is problematic

With enough data, a pattern can always be found, and the absence of a prior prediction makes it look like a discovery.

## How to detect it

- A single pattern is picked out from many possibilities.
- Criteria or groups are defined after the fact.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether the hypothesis was stated before the data were examined.
- Look for confirmation in new data.
