## Definition

Nguyen (2020) distinguishes the bubble, in which information is missing, from the echo chamber, in which one learns to distrust those outside it.

## Competing definitions and debates

The extent of the phenomenon online is contested, and many people also see opposing opinions. Bail and colleagues (2018) even observe that exposure to the opposing view can, under certain conditions, reinforce positions.

## Why it helps to discern

The distinction makes the remedy precise: for a bubble, one can offer new sources; for an echo chamber, trust must first be regained.
