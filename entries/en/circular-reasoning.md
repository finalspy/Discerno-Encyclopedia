## How it works

The conclusion reappears in another form among the premises: "this source is reliable, because it says so itself". Walton (1991) analyses this technique as a tactic that avoids having to prove the point under discussion.

## Why it is problematic

The reasoning seems sound because it is coherent, but it gives no independent reason.

## How to detect it

- The evidence offered depends on the thesis it is supposed to prove.
- Reformulated, the argument lands back on the same claim.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for a reason that can be checked without first accepting the conclusion.
- Replace the words with synonyms to see whether the argument goes round in circles.
