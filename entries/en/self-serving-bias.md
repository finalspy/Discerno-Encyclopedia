## How it works

Mezulis and colleagues (2004) find this bias in many studies, with differences across cultures and ages. Miller and Ross (1975) had however warned that part of the phenomenon may come from non-motivated reasons, such as initial expectations.

## Why it is problematic

This bias prevents people from learning from their mistakes. It also makes the reports presented by organizations and managers less reliable: successes are claimed, and failures are blamed on the context.

## How to detect it

- Merit is always internal, fault is always external.
- No failure is analyzed.

## How to protect yourself

- Apply the same criteria to successes and to failures.
- Keep a journal of your predictions and their outcomes.
