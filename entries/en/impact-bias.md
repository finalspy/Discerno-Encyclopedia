## How it works

Gilbert and colleagues (1998) show that we underestimate our capacity for adaptation: after a failure or a success, the effect fades faster than predicted.

## Why it is problematic

Promises of happiness and threats of unhappiness play on this exaggerated forecast of what one will feel.

## How to detect it

- "With this, your life will change."
- "You will never recover from it", without any other evidence.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask people who have already lived through the event.
- Remember your own past forecasts and how accurate they were.
