## How it works

Giving each side the same space seems fair. Boykoff and Boykoff (2004) show how this journalistic norm can overrepresent a minority position in a scientific field. This should not be confused with pluralism on questions of values, where there is no single factual answer.

## Why it is problematic

The public perceives a controversy where the evidence converges, and a maneuver can exploit this to delay a decision.

## How to detect it

- "Experts are divided", with no proportions.
- One person against one person, whatever the weight of the evidence.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for syntheses and the actual proportions.
- Distinguish a factual question from a choice of values.
