## How it works

The invoked group may be a nation, a community or a cause, whichever side one is on. Supporting the position becomes a proof of loyalty. Billig (1995) describes how national belonging is reproduced daily, through ordinary symbols.

## Why it is problematic

Disagreement over a measure can be presented as betrayal, and the question of whether the measure works disappears.

## How to detect it

- "Any true X will support…".
- Symbols replace data on effects.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether the measure really serves this group, and how one knows.
- Separate attachment to a group from agreement with a proposal.
