## How it works

The reasoning moves from "it could happen" to "it will happen". A mere possibility is treated as a certainty, without any estimate of its probability.

## Why it is problematic

Almost any fear or promise can be justified in this way.

## How to detect it

- "It will inevitably happen eventually."
- No estimate of the probability is given.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for the probability and its basis.
- Distinguish possible, probable and certain.
