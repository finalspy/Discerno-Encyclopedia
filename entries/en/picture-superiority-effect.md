## How it works

Paivio and Csapo (1973) explain this effect by dual coding: the image is recorded both visually and verbally.

## Why it is problematic

An image can stay in memory far longer than the text that corrects it. Out of context, it seems to prove what its caption says.

## How to detect it

- A striking image accompanies a claim it does not prove.
- The date and place of the image are not given.

## How to protect yourself

- Run a reverse image search.
- Read the caption as a claim to verify.
