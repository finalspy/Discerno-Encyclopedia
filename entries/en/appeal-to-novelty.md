## How it works

"New" or "revolutionary" replaces the comparison. The reasoning is the mirror image of the appeal to tradition: in both cases, the date stands in for an argument.

## Why it is problematic

The new has sometimes not yet been evaluated, and its limits are precisely unknown.

## How to detect it

- Novelty is the main argument.
- What already exists is dismissed as outdated in principle, without comparison.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask in what way it is better, and by what measure.
- Wait for independent results before important decisions.
