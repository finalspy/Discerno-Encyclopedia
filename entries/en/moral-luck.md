## Definition

Two drivers commit the same recklessness. One causes an accident, the other does not. The first is often judged far more harshly, although the only difference lies in chance. Williams and Nagel made it a philosophical problem.

## Competing definitions and debates

For some, this judgment is an error to be corrected. For others, outcomes legitimately count in responsibility. The question has no consensual answer.

## Why it helps to discern

The notion helps distinguish, within a judgment, what relates to conduct from what relates to outcome.
