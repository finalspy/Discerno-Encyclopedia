## How it works

Epley, Waytz and Cacioppo (2007) explain this tendency by three factors: our knowledge of humans, the need to make sense of our surroundings, and the need for social connection.

## Why it is problematic

An interface that says "I understand" can inspire trust unrelated to its abilities. Brands use it to create attachment.

## How to detect it

- A product or a tool speaks in the first person, with emotions.
- We attribute an intention to the system.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what the system actually does.
- Do not mistake fluent language for understanding.
