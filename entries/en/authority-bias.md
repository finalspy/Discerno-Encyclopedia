## How it works

Milgram (1963) shows that many participants follow an experimenter's instructions to the point of acts they disapprove of. Burger (2009) finds a similar tendency in a partial replication. The interpretation of these studies remains debated, but the role of status and context is well documented.

## Why it is problematic

A white coat, a title or a certificate can be enough to make a claim accepted outside the speaker's field of competence.

## How to detect it

- The title is put forward, not the evidence.
- The expertise invoked has no connection with the subject.

## How to protect yourself

- Check the field of competence and the state of knowledge.
- Ask whether you would accept the same statement without the title.
