## How it works

An expression receives two readings: an ordinary one for the majority, and a coded one for a group. Saul (2018) distinguishes explicit uses, understood as such by the group, from implicit uses, which act without the listener being aware of them.

## Why it is problematic

The message escapes criticism, because its author can always deny it. Conversely, seeing codes everywhere makes it possible to accuse anyone without evidence.

## How to detect it

- A phrase is repeated regularly by a group known to give it a specific meaning.
- The coded meaning is documented, not merely assumed.
- The reversal test applies: this technique exists in every camp.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask the author to clarify what they mean.
- Document the usage before accusing, and never conclude on a single word.
