## How it works

Weinstein (1980) asks students to estimate their chances of various events, relative to others. On average, they rate themselves as better placed, which cannot be true for everyone. Sharot (2011) presents a review of work on this bias.

## Why it is problematic

This bias can lead to neglecting prevention or accepting an overly risky investment. Some sellers exploit it: "why not you?"

## How to detect it

- "It only happens to others."
- Only the favorable scenario is described.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for the actual frequency in a comparable population.
- Explicitly plan for the unfavorable scenario.
