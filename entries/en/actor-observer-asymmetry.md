## How it works

Jones and Nisbett (1971) proposed this asymmetry, based on a difference of viewpoint and of information. Malle and colleagues (2007) qualify it: the gap exists, but it takes other forms than a simple contrast between situation and personality.

## Why it is problematic

The reasoning leads us to excuse our own side and blame others for the same conduct.

## How to detect it

- "I had to do it", but "he is just like that".
- The other person's situation is not considered.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what you would do in their place, with their constraints.
- Ask yourself the same question about yourself and about the other person.
