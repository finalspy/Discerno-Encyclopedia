## How it works

The process consists of denying established facts, rewriting past exchanges, or presenting the other person's reaction as excessive. Sweet (2019) analyzes it as a phenomenon linked to power relations within a relationship, not as a simple disagreement.

## Why it is problematic

The targeted person loses confidence in their ability to judge and depends more on the one who contradicts them. The word is sometimes used wrongly for any disagreement, which blurs its meaning.

## How to detect it

- "I never said that", despite a written record.
- Reactions are dismissed as madness or oversensitivity.
- A single disagreement is not enough: what matters is repetition and control.

## How to protect yourself

- Keep dated records.
- Check with people you trust.
- If control is involved, turn to professionals or support associations.
