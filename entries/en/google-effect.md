## How it works

Sparrow, Liu and Wegner (2011) observe that participants remember less well information they think they can retrieve later. However, one of the findings of this study was not replicated in the program led by Camerer and colleagues (2018): the scope of the effect remains uncertain.

## Why it is problematic

One can confuse knowing where to find information with understanding it.

## How to detect it

- One feels informed but cannot explain anything without a screen.

## How to protect yourself

- Check your understanding by explaining it without searching.
- Retain at least the basic concepts of a subject you want to judge.
