## How it works

Pronin and colleagues (2001) observe this asymmetry among friends and between groups: each person thinks they understand the other group better than the other understands it.

## Why it is problematic

One may believe one knows what an opponent really thinks, and refuse to listen to what they say about it.

## How to detect it

- "I know what they really think."
- What a group says about itself is dismissed out of hand.

## How to protect yourself

- Check your understanding with the people concerned.
- Ask yourself whether you would accept being talked about in this way.
