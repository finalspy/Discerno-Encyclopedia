## How it works

Loewenstein (2005) describes how a calm person poorly predicts what they will want in an intense emotional state, and conversely.

## Why it is problematic

A decision made under pressure is judged harshly, and one commits while believing one will resist a situation that will change one's mind. Sales and urgency techniques play on these states.

## How to detect it

- A decision is requested at a moment of strong emotion.
- A conduct is judged without regard to the person's state.

## How to protect yourself

- Make important decisions when calm, with a delay.
- Set your rules in advance for difficult situations.
