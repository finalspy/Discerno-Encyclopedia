## How it works

Slamecka and Graf (1978) show that words which participants had to complete are better remembered than words that were simply read.

## Why it is problematic

A conclusion "found" on one's own, based on carefully placed clues, can seem all the more correct. "Do your own research" can take advantage of this attachment.

## How to detect it

- A piece of content lets the reader take the final step without ever stating it.
- The feeling of having discovered something alone is used as proof.

## How to protect yourself

- Check your own deductions as you would check someone else's.
- Ask who prepared the clues.
