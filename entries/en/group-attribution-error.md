## How it works

Allison and Messick (1985) show that people infer members' opinions from a collective decision, even when the voting rule could have produced that result without a large majority.

## Why it is problematic

A vote, a statement or an isolated act becomes the supposed opinion of an entire country, party or community.

## How to detect it

- "Xs think that", based on a single decision.
- Internal division within the group is ignored.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for how opinions are distributed within the group.
- Distinguish the institution, its representatives and its members.
