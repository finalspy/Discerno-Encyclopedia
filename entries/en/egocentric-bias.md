## How it works

Ross and Sicoly (1979) ask spouses to estimate their share of shared tasks. The total of the estimates often exceeds 100%, partly because we remember better what we did ourselves.

## Why it is problematic

Conflicts arise from this sincere impression of doing more than the other person, and organizations can overestimate their role in an outcome.

## How to detect it

- Each party claims the largest share.
- The contributions of others are not described.

## How to protect yourself

- List each person's contributions explicitly.
- Ask the other person's opinion before concluding.
