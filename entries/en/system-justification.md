## How it works

Jost and Banaji (1994) propose this motivation, distinct from self-interest and group interest. Its scope is debated, particularly regarding disadvantaged people. No political position follows from it: any established order, whatever it is, can benefit from it.

## Why it is problematic

A situation may seem fair simply because it exists. Conversely, invoking this bias to dismiss all support for an institution would be an abuse.

## How to detect it

- An institution is defended because it exists, without any examination of its effects.
- "If things are this way, there must be a reason."

## How to protect yourself

- Ask which effects are observed, and for whom.
- Compare with other possible arrangements, on written criteria.
