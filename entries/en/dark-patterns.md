## How it works

Mathur and colleagues (2019) found them on many online shops: false scarcity, fees added at the end, difficult cancellation. Gray and colleagues (2018) offer a typology.

## Why it is problematic

The choice is steered by the design of the screen, without argument, and it can be hard to undo.

## How to detect it

- Subscribing takes one click, unsubscribing takes ten.
- Refusing is written in small print or in a guilt-inducing way.
- Boxes are pre-ticked.

## How to protect yourself

- Slow down at each confirmation screen.
- Check the total before paying.
- Report these practices to consumer protection authorities.
