## How it works

Schmidt (1994) shows that funny sentences are better recalled than serious ones, especially when they are mixed in with them.

## Why it is problematic

A meme stays in memory and is shared easily, even without an argument, and criticizing a joke can seem out of place.

## How to detect it

- A joke carries a factual claim.
- "It was just a joke", once the content is criticized.

## How to protect yourself

- Restate the claim it contains, seriously.
- Check before sharing, even if it is funny.
