## How it works

Spranca, Minsk and Baron (1991) show that, with equal consequences, a harmful action is judged more severely than an omission. Ritov and Baron (1990) describe a reluctance to act even when inaction carries a higher risk.

## Why it is problematic

Doing nothing can seem safe and free of responsibility, whereas inaction also has consequences.

## How to detect it

- Only the risks of the action are discussed.
- "At least I am not responsible for it."

## How to protect yourself

- Compare the consequences of both options, including doing nothing.
- Ask whether your judgment would change with identical results.
