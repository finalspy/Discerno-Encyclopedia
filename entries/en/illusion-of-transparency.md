## How it works

Gilovich, Savitsky and Medvec (1998) observe, for example, that people who lie believe their lie is more visible than it is, and that a hidden disgust seems more obvious to the person who feels it.

## Why it is problematic

One believes one has been understood when the message has not come across, and is surprised that others do not react.

## How to detect it

- "It was obvious", though nothing was said.
- A misunderstanding surprises both parties.

## How to protect yourself

- State explicitly what you think or expect.
- Ask the other person to restate what they understood.
