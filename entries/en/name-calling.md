## How it works

An insult or a defamatory word replaces the discussion. Da San Martino and colleagues (2019) retain this technique in their annotation of propaganda in press articles.

## Why it is problematic

The label stays in memory, and debating whether it is accurate already diverts attention from the substance.

## How to detect it

- A derogatory name is repeated in place of an argument.
- The word has a precise meaning, but is used without criteria.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what the label means and what justifies it.
- Describe acts rather than qualify people.
