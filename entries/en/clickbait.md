## How it works

Chen, Conroy and Rubin (2015) describe the signs of this technique. The headline creates curiosity by hiding the essential point, or an emotion that the article does not justify.

## Why it is problematic

Many people stop at the headline, and sharing can happen without reading.

## How to detect it

- "You will never guess…".
- The headline asserts what the article qualifies or contradicts.

## How to protect yourself

- Read the article before sharing it.
- Compare the headline with what is actually written.
