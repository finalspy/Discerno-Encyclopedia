## How it works

In the experiments by Kahneman, Knetsch and Thaler (1990), those who received a mug demanded a higher price for it than the price others were willing to pay for the same mug. The explanation and scope of this gap are still debated.

## Why it is problematic

Free trials and "try it at home" offers rely on this sense of ownership to make giving up harder.

## How to detect it

- An offer makes you "already" own something.
- A price is set according to attachment rather than the market.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask yourself how much you would pay to acquire it today.
- Note the end date of a free trial before starting it.
