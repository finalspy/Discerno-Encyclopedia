## How it works

Ross (1989) shows that people reconstruct their past based on what they are today and on what they believe about their own stability.

## Why it is problematic

A person can change their mind without noticing it, and deny having thought otherwise, in good faith.

## How to detect it

- "I have always thought that", contradicted by written traces.

## How to protect yourself

- Keep dated notes of your opinions, then reread them.
- Accept having changed your mind.
