## How it works

The fact may be real, but its magnitude is distorted by words or numbers: "catastrophe", "detail", a percentage with no base. This is a technique identified in the propaganda annotations of Da San Martino and colleagues (2019).

## Why it is problematic

The decision rests on a distorted scale, without necessarily containing any false information.

## How to detect it

- Superlatives, absolutes or diminutives without an order of magnitude.
- A relative change is given without its absolute value.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for the raw figures and the denominator.
- Compare with a reference case or period.
