## How it works

A bad argument does not prove that the conclusion is false: it may be true for other reasons. Refuting an argument only shows that it does not support the conclusion.

## Why it is problematic

This risk directly concerns the readers of such an encyclopedia: spotting a fallacy is not enough to win a debate or settle a question.

## How to detect it

- "You committed a fallacy, so you are wrong."
- Only the weakest version of a thesis is refuted.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for the strongest argument in favor of the thesis.
- State what has been refuted: the argument, not necessarily its conclusion.
