## How it works

Instead of answering, the interlocutor introduces another topic, often emotionally charged. Attention shifts and the initial question remains unanswered, without anyone having refuted it.

## Why it is problematic

The audience has the impression that an exchange has taken place, although the point under discussion has not advanced.

## How to detect it

- The reply talks about something else, with assurance.
- The new subject is more moving or easier than the first.
- In the end, one no longer knows what the question was.

## How to protect yourself

- Note the initial question and return to it calmly.
- Acknowledge that the other topic can be addressed, but separately.
