## How it works

Schkade and Kahneman (1998) compare declared life satisfaction in California and in the Midwest. Participants believe there is a gap because of the climate, but actual satisfaction is similar: the climate occupies the whole mind at the moment of judgment.

## Why it is problematic

A message can reduce a complex choice to a single criterion, the one that suits it.

## How to detect it

- A single argument takes up all the space.
- Everything comes down to a promise: "with this, everything will change".

## How to protect yourself

- List the other criteria that matter.
- Ask what would remain unchanged in daily life.
