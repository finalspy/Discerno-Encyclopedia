## How it works

MacLeod, Mathews and Tata (1986) observe that anxious people direct their attention more quickly towards threatening words. We notice more of what worries us, and this strengthens the worry.

## Why it is problematic

An information flow tailored to our interests can make a subject seem to occupy more space in the world than it does in our attention.

## How to detect it

- A subject suddenly seems to be everywhere since it has started to worry us.
- Headlines and images directly target our emotions.

## How to protect yourself

- Check the actual frequency with data.
- Vary your sources and limit alarming feeds.
