## How it works

Rogers, Kuiper and Kirker (1977) show that words processed by asking "Does this describe me?" are better remembered than words processed for their meaning or their form.

## Why it is problematic

A personalized message attracts more attention and is retained better, without being more accurate. Targeted advertising relies on this appeal.

## How to detect it

- "This concerns you", with no specific reason given.
- A text addresses the reader directly with generalities.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what the message says to someone else.
- Separate what touches us from what is demonstrated.
