## How it works

Karlsson, Loewenstein and Seppi (2009) observe that investors check their accounts less often when markets fall. Avoiding the information spares short-term discomfort.

## Why it is problematic

A problem grows because one does not look at it, and unwelcome information can be rejected without having been read.

## How to detect it

- The moment of checking a result is postponed.
- A topic is ruled out in advance: "I don't want to know about it."

## How to protect yourself

- Schedule one's checks in advance.
- Read the facts first, and reserve time to react.
