## How it works

Peltzman (1975) proposes this hypothesis about road safety. Hedlund (2000) analyzes it: compensation exists in some cases, but does not always cancel out the benefits. Its extent remains debated depending on the field.

## Why it is problematic

The argument is sometimes used to reject a protection outright, without measuring the net effect.

## How to detect it

- "It doesn't prevent anything, so what's the point?", without measurement.
- A feeling of safety accompanies a risk-taking.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for the measured net effect of the measure.
- Watch one's own relaxation after a protection.
