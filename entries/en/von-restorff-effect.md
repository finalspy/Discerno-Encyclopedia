## How it works

Hunt (1995) revisits the work of von Restorff (1933): a different item in a list is better memorized, including when it was not expected.

## Why it is problematic

An offer, a word or a figure that is highlighted attracts choice and memory, regardless of its actual merit.

## How to detect it

- An item is isolated by color or size.
- The rest, including the conditions, goes unnoticed.

## How to protect yourself

- Also read what is not highlighted.
- Ask why this item was singled out.
