## How it works

Rogers (1962) points out this bias in research on the adoption of innovations itself: studies focus mainly on successes, and assume that rejecting a novelty is an error.

## Why it is problematic

Technological promises can pass without evaluation, and their critics can seem backward.

## How to detect it

- Limits and costs are absent from the presentation.
- Doubt is presented as a fear of progress.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for an independent evaluation, including failures.
- Compare with existing solutions.
