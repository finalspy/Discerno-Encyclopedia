## Definition

Peter and Hull (1969) set it out in a satirical book: since one is promoted for success in the current position, one eventually reaches a position for which one lacks the competence, and remains there.

Status: satirical in origin, partly confirmed by studies.

## Competing definitions and debates

Benson, Li and Shue (2019) find, on salespeople from 131 companies, results consistent with the principle: the best salespeople are promoted to managers although other criteria predicted success in management better. The principle does not say that managers are incompetent in general.

## Why it helps to discern

Succeeding in one field does not prove competence in another. The same confusion leads to credit being given to someone outside their specialty.
