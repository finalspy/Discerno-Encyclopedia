## How it works

Fredrickson and Kahneman (1993) observe that ratings of films, whether pleasant or not, depend little on their length, and more on their peak moments and their ending.

## Why it is problematic

A long suffering can seem less serious than a short but far more intense one. The result can be poorly reasoned trade-offs.

## How to detect it

- An assessment never mentions duration.

## How to protect yourself

- Measure duration explicitly, in addition to intensity.
