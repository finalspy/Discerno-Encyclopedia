## How it works

Mitchell and colleagues (1997) follow people before, during and after a trip or an outing. The experience is judged better after the fact than it was while being lived.

## Why it is problematic

Nostalgia can be mobilized to sell a product or to defend the idea that "things were better before", without any real comparison.

## How to detect it

- An idealized past is used as an argument, without figures.
- The difficulties of the period are not mentioned.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for period data or accounts.
- Compare the present with the real past, not with one's memory of it.
