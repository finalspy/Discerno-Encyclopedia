## How it works

Before the other person even speaks, we announce that they are biased, paid or incompetent. Their words are then interpreted through this label, and any reply seems to confirm what was said.

## Why it is problematic

The public judges a speech before hearing it. Pointing out a documented conflict of interest remains legitimate, provided it is proven and the substance is also discussed.

## How to detect it

- A warning about the person comes before any argument.
- The accusation is unsourced, or makes any reply look suspicious.

## How to protect yourself

- Listen to the argument and judge it on its evidence.
- Ask for the evidence behind the announced suspicion.
