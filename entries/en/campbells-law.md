## Definition

The psychologist Donald T. Campbell stated it in relation to the evaluation of public policies (Campbell, 1979): a quantitative indicator that has become a stake in decisions is subject to pressures that corrupt it, and it ends up distorting the practices it was supposed to observe.

Status: an empirical observation, drawn from the evaluation of social programmes.

## Competing definitions and debates

It is close to Goodhart's law, formulated at around the same time in economics. Campbell (1979) does not conclude that measurement should be abandoned: he recommends several indicators and qualitative methods alongside the figures.

## Why it helps to discern

When a success rate, a ranking or an outcome statistic is linked to a reward or a sanction, the question is not only its value but how it may have been obtained.
