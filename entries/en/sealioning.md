## How it works

Each request seems reasonable taken on its own: "Do you have a source?" Repeated endlessly and without regard for the answers, they wear the other person down and make them look aggressive when they finally tire of it.

## Why it is problematic

The polite tone serves as a cover. Asking for proof remains legitimate: it is the repetition without listening that constitutes the tactic.

## How to detect it

- Questions that have already been answered are asked again.
- The sources provided are ignored.
- The request continues across several threads or over several days.

## How to protect yourself

- Answer once, give the source, then close the exchange.
- Distinguish the sincere interlocutor from the one who does not read the replies.
