## How it works

Antiquity stands in for an argument. A long-lasting practice may have good reasons to exist, but it is those reasons that should be examined: duration alone does not establish them. The opposite error also exists, the appeal to novelty.

## Why it is problematic

The reasoning avoids examining a practice's current effects and the conditions that have changed.

## How to detect it

- "We have always done it this way."
- No reason is given beyond its age.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what the practice is for today.
- Compare with other options, without bias towards the old or the new.
