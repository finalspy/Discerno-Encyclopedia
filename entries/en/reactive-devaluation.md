## How it works

Ross and Stillinger (1991) describe this obstacle to negotiation: the same proposal is judged less favorably when attributed to the opponent. A concession may then seem suspicious, or worthless.

## Why it is problematic

Possible agreements fail, and public debate reduces to who is proposing rather than what is proposed.

## How to detect it

- A proposal is judged differently depending on its author.
- An opponent's concession is immediately taken as a trap.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate a proposal without knowing its author.
- Ask what one would think of it if one's own side had made it.
