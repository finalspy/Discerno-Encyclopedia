## How it works

Paul and Matthews (2016) named this model, describing four traits: high volume and multiple channels, rapidity and repetition, no commitment to reality, and lack of consistency. The study starts from a historical case, but the technique described is specific to no single camp.

## Why it is problematic

Verification cannot keep pace, repetition makes messages familiar, and weariness can lead people to stop believing anyone.

## How to detect it

- Contradictory versions are circulated almost at the same time.
- New claims are made before the previous ones have been verified.

## How to protect yourself

- Reduce your exposure, and follow sources that correct their errors.
- Refuse to conclude that "everything is equally valid".
