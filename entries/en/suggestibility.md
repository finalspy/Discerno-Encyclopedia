## How it works

Ceci and Bruck (1993) review research on child witnesses: repeated, leading questions can change what children report. These effects are not limited to children, and they do not mean that children lie.

## Why it is problematic

The way a question is asked can produce the desired answer, in good faith.

## How to detect it

- Questions are repeated until an answer is obtained.
- The account shifts toward what the questioner expects.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask open, neutral questions.
- In court or in an investigation, rely on recognized protocols.
