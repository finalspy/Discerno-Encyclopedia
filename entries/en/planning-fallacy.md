## How it works

Buehler, Griffin and Ross (1994) ask students when they will finish a project. Most exceed their estimate, including the one made for the unfavorable case. They focus on the plan rather than on past experience.

## Why it is problematic

A promised deadline or cost can seem sincere while being based only on the ideal scenario.

## How to detect it

- A schedule with no margin and no reference to comparable projects.
- Overruns each time attributed to particular unforeseen events.

## How to protect yourself

- Start from the actual duration of similar projects.
- Ask an outsider to make the estimate.
