## Definition

Jowett and O'Donnell (1986) describe a deliberate and systematic attempt to shape perceptions in the service of the propagandist. Ellul (1965) analyzes it as a phenomenon specific to mass societies, which cannot be reduced to lies.

## Competing definitions and debates

Definitions diverge on the role of intent, on the place of truth, and on the boundary with advertising or public communication. The word is also used as an insult to discredit a message.

## Why it helps to discern

Examining the techniques used, rather than attaching a label, makes it possible to treat all sides in the same way.
