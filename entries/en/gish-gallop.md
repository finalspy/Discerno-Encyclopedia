## How it works

Refuting a claim takes longer than making it. By multiplying claims, one creates an impression of a solid case, and each point left unanswered is taken as conceded.

## Why it is problematic

Quantity is mistaken for quality, and the opponent appears to be struggling whatever their arguments.

## How to detect it

- Many claims, little developed and little sourced.
- The speaker switches to another point as soon as one is challenged.

## How to protect yourself

- Choose the central claim and examine it thoroughly.
- Say clearly that the number of arguments does not measure their strength.
- Ask the other person for their best piece of evidence, just one.
