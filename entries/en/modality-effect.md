## How it works

Penney (1989) summarizes this work: when heard aloud, the last items are better recalled than when read. The form of a message thus affects memory.

## Why it is problematic

This technical result is a reminder that the same content is not received in the same way in writing and when heard.

## How to detect it

- Essential information is given only orally, very quickly.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for a written version of important information.
