## How it works

Rozenblit and Keil (2002) ask participants to rate their understanding of everyday objects, to explain them, then to rate themselves again: the rating drops. Fernbach and colleagues (2013) observe that explaining the mechanism of a public policy also moderates certain positions.

## Why it is problematic

Strong opinions can rest on an assumed understanding, and people are reluctant to look further.

## How to detect it

- An explanation stays at the surface as soon as one asks "how, exactly?".
- Certainty comes with no detail about the mechanism.

## How to protect yourself

- Try to explain step by step, in writing.
- Ask your interlocutor to explain the mechanism rather than their reasons.
