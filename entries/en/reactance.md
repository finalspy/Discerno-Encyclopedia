## How it works

Brehm (1966) describes a motivational state that pushes one to restore a lost freedom. Steindl and colleagues (2015) review recent research. An injunctive tone can therefore produce the opposite of the intended effect.

## Why it is problematic

Reactance can be exploited: "what they don't want you to know" gives appeal to content, without evidence.

## How to detect it

- A real or assumed prohibition is used as an argument.
- One feels like embracing something mainly because one is told not to.

## How to protect yourself

- Separate what one thinks of the content from the way it is imposed.
- Ask oneself what one would choose without this pressure.
