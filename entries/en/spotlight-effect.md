## How it works

In an experiment by Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky (2000), students wearing an embarrassing T-shirt clearly overestimate the number of people who noticed it.

## Why it is problematic

Fear of being watched can prevent someone from asking a question or expressing disagreement, and it can create the belief in a collective attention that does not exist.

## How to detect it

- The fear of being judged holds back someone from speaking up.
- People believe everyone has noticed a small mistake.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask others what they actually saw.
- Remember that everyone is mostly preoccupied with themselves.
