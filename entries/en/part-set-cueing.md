## How it works

Slamecka (1968) observes that giving some of the words of a list does not help recall the others, and can even make them less accessible.

## Why it is problematic

A form or a suggestion can steer what one remembers. The options offered can block those one would otherwise have found.

## How to detect it

- A list of examples comes before the question.

## How to protect yourself

- Write down your ideas before reading the proposals.
