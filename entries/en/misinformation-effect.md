## How it works

Loftus, Miller and Burns (1978) show images of an accident, then slip a false detail into a question. Later, some of the participants choose that detail. Loftus (2005) reviews thirty years of work on this subject.

## Why it is problematic

The account given by the media, a relative or a group can transform what one has oneself seen.

## How to detect it

- Witnesses have exchanged accounts before being questioned.
- The memory changes after reading reports.

## How to protect yourself

- Write down what you saw before reading other accounts.
- Compare with the original recordings.
