## How it works

"Why are you hiding the truth?" already assumes that it is being hidden. Loftus and Palmer (1974) also show that the verb used in a question can change estimates and memories of the same event.

## Why it is problematic

The presupposition passes unexamined, and refusing to answer looks like a confession.

## How to detect it

- The question contains an unproven accusation.
- Yes and no both confirm the presupposition.

## How to protect yourself

- Answer the presupposition first: "What makes you say that...?"
- Rephrase the question in neutral terms.
