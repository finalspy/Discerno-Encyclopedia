## How it works

Raghubir and Srivastava (2009) show this preference in several experiments: a large bill is harder to "break", and small change is spent faster.

## Why it is problematic

The same presentation mechanism can make a repeated expense seem smaller, for example with tokens, credits or micro-purchases.

## How to detect it

- A price is converted into tokens or in-app units.
- Small amounts repeat, with no total displayed.

## How to protect yourself

- Convert to euros and add up over the month.
- Set a budget before starting.
