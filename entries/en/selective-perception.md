## How it works

Hastorf and Cantril (1954) had students from two rival universities watch film of a football game. Each group "saw" clearly more fouls committed by the other team.

## Why it is problematic

Faced with the same images or the same facts, two opposing camps can sincerely have seen two different events.

## How to detect it

- Each camp summarizes the same video in opposite terms.
- People mainly notice the mistakes of the other side.

## How to protect yourself

- Describe the facts before interpreting them.
- Count the same elements for both sides.
