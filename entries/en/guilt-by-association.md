## How it works

A shared point, sometimes a slight one, is enough to transfer a judgment: "this idea is also defended by X". No examination of the idea itself is needed. The SemEval-2023 taxonomy lists this procedure among persuasion techniques.

## Why it is problematic

This emotional link replaces the argument, and no one can guard against everyone who shares one of their ideas.

## How to detect it

- The comparison with a repellent figure or group stands in for an answer.
- The link mentioned has no connection to the subject.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask how this link changes the value of the argument.
- Apply the inversion test to your own ideas.
