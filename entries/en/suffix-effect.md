## How it works

Crowder and Morton (1969) explain it through a very brief auditory memory, which the following sound replaces.

## Why it is problematic

It is a laboratory effect, of little use for judging a text. It simply shows that a fragile spoken message can be erased by what follows it.

## How to detect it

- A spoken instruction is immediately followed by another message.

## How to protect yourself

- Allow a pause of silence, or ask for a repetition, after an important piece of information.
