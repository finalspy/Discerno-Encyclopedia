## How it works

McDaniel and Einstein (1986) find this advantage mainly when bizarre elements are mixed with ordinary ones: distinctiveness is what matters.

## Why it is problematic

A striking story is remembered and shared better, without being any more true or important.

## How to detect it

- A surprising fact circulates without a source.
- The unusual detail takes up more room than the substance.

## How to protect yourself

- Verify the most astonishing facts first.
- Ask whether the fact matters, or is merely memorable.
