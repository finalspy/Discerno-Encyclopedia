## How it works

The meta-analysis by Freling and colleagues (2020) concludes that stories can weigh more than statistics in judgments, especially when the topic is personally or emotionally involving. A testimony can be true without being representative.

## Why it is problematic

One case of recovery, success or scandal can make us forget all those that are not told.

## How to detect it

- "It worked for me."
- A testimony is set against a study, without discussing what each is worth.

## How to protect yourself

- Respect the story, then look for the overall data.
- Ask what happened to those who do not testify.
