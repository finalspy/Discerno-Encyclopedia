## How it works

Svenson (2008) shows that intuition poorly tracks the fact that the time saved depends non-linearly on speed. Going from 30 to 50 km/h over 10 km saves 8 minutes, while going from 110 to 130 km/h saves less than a minute.

## Why it is problematic

This bias affects decisions about speed, productivity and tools that promise to "go faster".

## How to detect it

- The speed gain is given, but not the time actually saved.

## How to protect yourself

- Do the calculation in minutes, on a real trip or task.
