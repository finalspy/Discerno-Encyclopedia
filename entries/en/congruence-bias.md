## How it works

Wason (1960) asks participants to guess a rule behind sequences of three numbers. Many propose sequences that match their own idea, instead of looking for those that could contradict it.

## Why it is problematic

An inquiry can seem rigorous while testing only a single hypothesis.

## How to detect it

- No test could prove the hypothesis wrong.
- The other explanations are not considered.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask which result would make you abandon the idea.
- Test at least one competing hypothesis.
