## How it works

Zwicky (2006) popularized the term. After a first encounter, selective attention detects each new occurrence, and confirmation bias keeps those while forgetting the others.

## Why it is problematic

One can conclude that a phenomenon is growing, or that a coincidence has meaning, without any measurement.

## How to detect it

- "We keep seeing it everywhere lately", with no data.
- The feeling begins just after discovering the subject.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare with data over several years.
- Remember that it may be attention that has changed, not the world.
