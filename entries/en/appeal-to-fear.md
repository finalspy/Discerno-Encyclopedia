## How it works

The message puts a danger forward and presents one action as the only way out. Tannenbaum and colleagues (2015) conclude that fear messages can change attitudes and behaviors. A real risk can legitimately be pointed out: the problem arises when fear replaces the evidence or inflates the danger.

## Why it is problematic

Fear shortens the time spent checking and makes a proposed solution more acceptable than it would be with a cool head.

## How to detect it

- The danger is described with no probability or order of magnitude.
- A single solution is offered, with urgency.
- The images or words are stronger than the data.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask how likely the risk is, and for whom.
- Look for the other possible responses.
- Tell a sourced warning apart from a staging.
