## How it works

Kahneman and Tversky (1973) describe confidence based on the consistency of a narrative, even when that information predicts poorly. Kahneman and Klein (2009) specify that reliable expertise requires a regular environment and feedback on one's results.

## Why it is problematic

An overconfident commentary with a coherent story can persuade without ever having made good predictions.

## How to detect it

- A very confident forecast comes with no record of results.
- The narrative is coherent, but the data are sparse.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for the track record of past forecasts.
- Check whether the field offers quick and regular feedback.
