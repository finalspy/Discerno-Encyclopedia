## How it works

Kahneman and colleagues (1993) had participants plunge a hand into cold water. They then preferred to repeat the longer trial, whose ending was less painful.

## Why it is problematic

A pleasant ending can make one forget the rest, which a service or a campaign can exploit.

## How to detect it

- A flattering gesture right at the end of a journey.
- The evaluation is requested right after the best moment.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate the whole experience, taking notes as you go.
- Separate the overall assessment from the final impression.
