## How it works

Kruger and Gilovich (1999) study couples and other partners who estimate their share of responsibility. Each one expects the other to claim more credit than they actually do.

## Why it is problematic

Attributing bad motives to others spares one from listening to their arguments and can make agreement harder.

## How to detect it

- Every position is explained by an assumed interest.
- "He says that because he stands to gain", with no evidence.

## How to protect yourself

- Separate the assumed motive from the value of the argument.
- Check your suspicions when possible.
