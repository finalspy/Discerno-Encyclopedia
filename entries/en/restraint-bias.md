## How it works

Nordgren and colleagues (2009) observe that people who believe they are in control of themselves expose themselves more to temptation and give in more often.

## Why it is problematic

Platforms and games can take advantage of this confidence to increase exposure. "I can stop whenever I want" is not evidence.

## How to detect it

- "I manage", without a measured limit.
- One voluntarily places oneself in the situation to be avoided.

## How to protect yourself

- Act on the environment, for example by turning off notifications.
- Measure one's actual use.
