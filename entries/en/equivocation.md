## How it works

Aristotle already described these refutations based on the double meaning of words. A term such as "theory" or "natural" changes meaning between the premises and the conclusion, without the shift being signaled.

## Why it is problematic

The conclusion seems to follow logically, when the reasoning in fact concerns two different notions.

## How to detect it

- A key word is never defined.
- Replacing the word with its definition makes the argument unsound.

## How to protect yourself

- Have the meaning of the word specified at each step.
- Rewrite the argument with distinct terms.
