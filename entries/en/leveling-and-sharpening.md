## How it works

Allport and Postman (1947) describe the transmission of a rumor: the story gets shorter, certain elements become more salient, and the whole adapts to the expectations of the people who pass it on.

## Why it is problematic

A nuanced piece of information becomes spectacular as it is shared, without any relay having lied deliberately.

## How to detect it

- A nuanced scientific article becomes a categorical headline.
- The shared version is sharper than the source.

## How to protect yourself

- Trace the story back to its original source.
- Compare the headline, the summary and the text.
