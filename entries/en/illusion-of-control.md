## How it works

Langer (1975) shows that features typical of skill situations, such as choice or familiarity, increase confidence in chance situations. In one of her experiments, participants who had chosen their lottery ticket demanded more money to give it up.

## Why it is problematic

Methods for gambling, trading or well-being can sell a feeling of mastery with no demonstrated effect.

## How to detect it

- A method promises to control a largely random outcome.
- Success is credited to the method, failure to how it was applied.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare with a group that does not use the method.
- Ask what share of the outcome depends on chance.
