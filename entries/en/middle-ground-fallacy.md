## How it works

The compromise seems reasonable on principle. Yet on a factual question, the answer may lie entirely on one side. The middle can also be shifted: it is enough to defend a more extreme position to move the average.

## Why it is problematic

A false position gains a share of credit merely by existing. Compromise remains valuable for arbitrating interests, but it does not say what is true.

## How to detect it

- "The truth is surely somewhere between the two", without examination.
- The word "moderate" stands in for an argument.

## How to protect yourself

- Separate facts, which must be verified, from choices, which must be negotiated.
- Ask whether the middle would have shifted with other starting positions.
