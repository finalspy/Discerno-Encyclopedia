## How it works

A team of good players is not necessarily a good team (composition). A member of a rich group is not necessarily rich (division). What holds at one level does not automatically carry over to another.

## Why it is problematic

The reasoning leads to judging a person by the statistics of their group, or a system by a few local successes.

## How to detect it

- A direct move from the group to the individual, or the reverse.
- An average is applied to a specific person.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether the property really carries over from one level to the other.
- Treat individuals and groups separately.
