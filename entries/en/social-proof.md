## How it works

When uncertain, people observe what others do. Milgram and colleagues (1969) show that the larger the group looking up in a street, the more passersby did the same. The technique displays figures, opinions or testimonials to exploit this reflex.

## Why it is problematic

Popularity signals can be bought, selected or fabricated, and popularity does not prove quality.

## How to detect it

- "Thousands of customers", with no source.
- Very similar or uniformly excellent reviews.
- Accounts amplifying the same message at the same time.

## How to protect yourself

- Find out how the figure was obtained.
- Also read the average and negative reviews.
- Separate what is popular from what is established.
