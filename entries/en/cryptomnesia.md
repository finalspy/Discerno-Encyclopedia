## How it works

Brown and Murphy (1989) reproduce in the laboratory unintentional plagiarism: participants put forward as new ideas that they had already heard.

## Why it is problematic

One can believe in good faith to have invented an idea, which complicates attribution and can fuel disputes.

## How to detect it

- An "original idea" has already been expressed within the same group.

## How to protect yourself

- Check whether the idea already exists before claiming it.
- Note your sources as you go.
