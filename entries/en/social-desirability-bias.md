## How it works

Crowne and Marlowe (1960) propose a scale to measure this tendency to present oneself in a favorable light. Self-reported answers can be distorted by it, especially on sensitive topics.

## Why it is problematic

A poll can give a distorted picture of behaviors, whether on voting, health or money, and a result can be read beyond what it actually measures.

## How to detect it

- A survey covers a sensitive topic without methodological precautions.
- Stated answers and measured behaviors diverge.

## How to protect yourself

- Look at the exact question and the way the data was collected.
- Compare with behavioral data.
