## How it works

Real chance produces runs and clusters. Gilovich (1991) explains that people wrongly expect chance to produce a regular alternation, so that clumps seem to call for an explanation.

## Why it is problematic

A group of cases on a map or over time can seem to have a hidden cause. It may have one, but this has to be verified.

## How to detect it

- A cluster is given as evidence, without a comparison.
- The area or period is defined after the fact.

## How to protect yourself

- Calculate what chance would produce.
- Test the pattern on other data.
