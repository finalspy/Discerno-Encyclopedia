## How it works

Walker and Vul (2014) propose that the eye summarizes the group as an average, and that each face is then perceived as closer to that average. The effect observed is modest.

## Why it is problematic

It is a reminder that our judgments depend on presentation, not only on what is presented, something advertising imagery knows how to exploit.

## How to detect it

- A group image is used to make a person or product look good.

## How to protect yourself

- Assess each element separately.
