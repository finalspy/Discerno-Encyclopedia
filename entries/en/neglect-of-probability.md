## How it works

Rottenstreich and Hsee (2001) show that the response to an emotionally charged outcome depends little on its probability. Sunstein (2002) draws consequences for prevention policy: the worst scenario fills the whole space.

## Why it is problematic

A rare but frightening risk can mobilize more than a frequent but ordinary one.

## How to detect it

- The worst-case scenario is described, without a probability.
- The discussion ignores more frequent risks.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask how frequent it is and compare with other risks.
- Distinguish severity from probability: both are needed.
