## How it works

Strunk, Lopez and DeRubeis (2006) observe that people with depressive symptoms make unrealistic negative predictions about their own lives. When this tendency weighs on daily life, professional support can help.

## Why it is problematic

Pessimism can seem clear-sighted, without being any more accurate than another bias, and it can discourage useful action.

## How to detect it

- The worst scenario is presented as the most realistic.
- Good news is treated as exceptions.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare one's forecasts with what actually happens.
- Look for observed frequencies.
