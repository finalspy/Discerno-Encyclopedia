## How it works

Hsee (1998) presents a small expensive gift and a large cheap one. Seen separately, the first is judged by its rank within its category, and it can be preferred to an objectively more costly option. When seen side by side, the preference can reverse.

## Why it is problematic

The mode of presentation changes the choice: a small high-end item can seem more valuable than an ordinary large one.

## How to detect it

- An offer is presented without a point of comparison.
- A product is "the best in its small category".

## How to protect yourself

- Compare the options side by side.
- Return to absolute values.
