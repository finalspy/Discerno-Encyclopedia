## How it works

What is presented as rare seems more valuable. Worchel and colleagues (1975) observe that cookies are rated higher when they come from an almost empty jar. Cialdini (1984) makes it a general lever of influence.

## Why it is problematic

Urgency reduces the time available for comparison, and the announced scarcity may be fictitious.

## How to detect it

- Countdown timers, "only 2 left in stock", "last chance".
- The offer comes back after having been announced as over.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether one would want the item without the announced limit.
- Check the price and availability elsewhere or later.
- Give oneself a fixed delay before buying.
