## How it works

Brown and McNeill (1966) bring about this state by giving definitions of rare words. Participants often recall the first letter or the number of syllables.

## Why it is problematic

This phenomenon has nothing manipulative about it. It simply shows that the impression of knowing and the act of recall are two distinct things.

## How to detect it

- One is certain of knowing, without being able to say it.

## How to protect yourself

- Move on to something else: the word often comes back afterwards.
- Check before treating a memory as accurate.
