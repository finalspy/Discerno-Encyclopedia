## How it works

A simple explanation is easier to remember and to pass on. Rozenblit and Keil (2002) show that people often overestimate their own understanding of complex mechanisms, which makes such explanations all the more appealing.

## Why it is problematic

The problem seems easy to solve, and the culprit easy to name. The proposed solutions risk missing their target.

## How to detect it

- "It's all because of…".
- No other factor is examined, nor any share attributed to each.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask which other factors play a part, and what share each has.
- Be wary of a single cause that suits the person proposing it.
