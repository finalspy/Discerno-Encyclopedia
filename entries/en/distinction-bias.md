## How it works

Hsee and Zhang (2004) show that joint evaluation magnifies quantitative differences, whereas one would hardly notice them when living with a single option.

## Why it is problematic

Detailed comparison sheets can push people to pay more for a difference with no real effect.

## How to detect it

- A table highlights small gaps.
- The criterion matters only because it is displayed.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether you would notice the difference in real use.
- Set your criteria before reading comparisons.
