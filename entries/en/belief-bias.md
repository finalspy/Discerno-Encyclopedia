## How it works

Evans, Barston and Pollard (1983) present valid and invalid syllogisms, with conclusions that are either credible or not. Participants more readily accept an invalid reasoning whose conclusion seems true to them.

## Why it is problematic

A weak argument gets through if it leads to what one already thinks, and a good argument can be rejected for the opposite reason.

## How to detect it

- An argument is judged on its conclusion alone.
- One's own side benefits from a less demanding reading.

## How to protect yourself

- Replace the content with letters and check the logic.
- Examine with the same care an argument whose conclusion you approve of.
