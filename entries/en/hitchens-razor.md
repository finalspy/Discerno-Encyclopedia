## Definition

The formula, popularized by Hitchens (2007), places the burden of proof on the one who asserts. Someone who doubts does not have to demonstrate that an unsupported claim is false.

Status: a methodological principle about the burden of proof, not a finding.

## Competing definitions and debates

"Reject" means not taking as given, not proving the opposite: a claim without evidence may prove true later. The principle is sometimes turned around to dismiss claims that have evidence which one has simply not consulted.

## Why it helps to discern

It answers the reversal of the burden of proof ("prove to me that it is false") and reminds us that one does not have to refute what was never supported.
