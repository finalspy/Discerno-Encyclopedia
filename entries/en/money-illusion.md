## How it works

Shafir, Diamond and Tversky (1997) show that people often evaluate a transaction in nominal terms. A pay rise can be satisfying even if it remains below inflation.

## Why it is problematic

Comparisons of prices, salaries or budgets across periods can be misleading, in either direction.

## How to detect it

- Amounts from several years are compared without adjustment.
- "Historic record", in nominal value.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for figures in constant euros.
- Relate amounts to income or GDP.
