## How it works

A real resemblance, often superficial, is used to conclude that two facts have the same gravity or call for the same judgment. Scale, intent, power to act and consequences are set aside.

## Why it is problematic

The technique can downplay a serious fact or inflate a minor one, whichever side is targeted.

## How to detect it

- "It's exactly the same", with no precise comparison.
- The resemblance selected has no bearing on the question.

## How to protect yourself

- List resemblances and differences, then see which ones matter.
- Compare orders of magnitude.
