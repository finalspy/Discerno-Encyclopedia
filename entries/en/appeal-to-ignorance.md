## How it works

The absence of evidence is turned into evidence. Walton (1996) notes that such reasoning can be reasonable when a serious search should have found something, but that it becomes faulty when nobody really looked.

## Why it is problematic

It makes almost any unverifiable claim acceptable.

## How to detect it

- "Nobody has proven otherwise."
- The other person is required to refute what has never been established.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask whether evidence should have been found, and who looked for it.
- Accept answering "we don't know".
