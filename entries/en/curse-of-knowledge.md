## How it works

Camerer, Loewenstein and Weber (1989) show that better-informed participants cannot ignore what they know, even when this costs them money.

## Why it is problematic

Experts can be unintelligible without meaning to be. This lack of clarity can be taken for concealment, or conversely for depth.

## How to detect it

- Acronyms and concepts are never explained.
- "It's simple", said of something that is not simple for the audience.

## How to protect yourself

- Test your explanation on a novice.
- Ask an expert for a concrete example.
