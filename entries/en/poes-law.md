## Definition

Formulated in 2005 on an online forum by the participant whose name it bears, in discussions about creationism, the law has been generalized to any extreme position. In writing, without tone or explicit signs, a reader cannot tell whether an outrageous text is serious or ironic.

Status: an observation about online communication, not measured.

## Competing definitions and debates

Aikin (2013) links it to group polarization: the further the camps move apart, the more the other side's positions look caricatured, and the more a caricature looks plausible. The law therefore describes the reader as much as the text.

## Why it helps to discern

A shocking screenshot can be a parody taken out of context, and a parody can be taken at face value. Before reacting or sharing, look for the source, the account and the original context.
