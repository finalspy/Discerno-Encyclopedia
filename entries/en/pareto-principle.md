## Definition

Economist Vilfredo Pareto observed at the end of the 19th century that a small part of the population held most of the land in Italy. The idea was later generalized, in quality management, into an 80/20 rule.

Status: an approximate empirical rule. The 80/20 ratio is not a constant.

## Competing definitions and debates

This kind of unequal distribution corresponds to so-called "power-law" distributions, which are common but not universal; Newman (2005) explains how to identify them and why they are often asserted wrongly on too little data. Depending on the case, the proportion may be 90/10, 60/40, or unrelated to this shape.

## Why it helps to discern

A figure such as "20% of the Xs cause 80% of the Ys" must come from measured data, not from the rule itself. Ask for the source of the proportion and for how it was calculated.
