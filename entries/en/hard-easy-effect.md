## How it works

Lichtenstein and Fischhoff (1977) compare confidence and correct answers according to the difficulty of the questions. On difficult questions, the gap in confidence is more pronounced.

## Why it is problematic

Confidence is all the less reliable as the subject is difficult, yet these are the subjects that call for the most caution.

## How to detect it

- High certainty on a very complex question.
- Unnecessary doubt on a simple, well-established question.

## How to protect yourself

- Rate your confidence as a percentage, then check.
- Increase your caution as the subject becomes more complicated.
