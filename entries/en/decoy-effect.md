## How it works

Huber, Payne and Puto (1982) add to a choice an option clearly worse than one of the two others. The share of the option that dominates it rises, although nothing has changed between the two original options.

## Why it is problematic

A price list can be built to steer the choice toward the offer the seller prefers.

## How to detect it

- An offer is almost as expensive as another, but clearly worse.
- An "ideal" option looks obvious only by comparison.

## How to protect yourself

- Mentally remove the unattractive option, then choose.
- Start from your needs rather than from the table provided.
