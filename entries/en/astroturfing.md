## How it works

Accounts, reviews or associations that appear independent spread a coordinated message. Kovic and his colleagues (2018) define the phenomenon by the concealment of the real organizer. The public believes it sees a widely shared opinion.

## Why it is problematic

The apparent support is manufactured, and public debate rests on a false picture of what people think.

## How to detect it

- Identical wording published at the same time by different accounts.
- Recent accounts with little personal activity.
- Funding or organizer cannot be found.

## How to protect yourself

- Find out who funds and who organizes.
- Compare with surveys whose method is published.
- Report coordinated networks to the platforms.
