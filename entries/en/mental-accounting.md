## How it works

Thaler (1985) describes separate mental accounts: an unexpected gain is spent more easily than a salary, and the same sum does not weigh the same depending on the budget line where it is filed.

## Why it is problematic

An offer can be presented so that it passes through a less closely watched account: "only a few euros a day", "for leisure".

## How to detect it

- A price is broken down into small units.
- A bonus or a credit encourages spending more.

## How to protect yourself

- Relate each expense to the total budget.
- Ask whether you would make the same choice with your salary.
