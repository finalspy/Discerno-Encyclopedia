## How it works

Aronson and Mills (1959) observe that female participants subjected to a more severe initiation liked a group that was in fact dull more. The explanation offered comes from the cognitive dissonance described by Festinger (1957).

## Why it is problematic

Costly rites, expensive training or demanding commitments make it harder to recognize that one was wrong or that the group is disappointing.

## How to detect it

- A group or method demands increasing sacrifices.
- The effort already invested serves as the argument for continuing.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate the outcome independently of what it cost.
- Ask for the opinion of people outside the group.
