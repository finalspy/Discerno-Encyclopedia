## How it works

Festinger (1954) explains that people evaluate themselves by comparing themselves with others. Garcia, Song and Tesser (2010) observe that people are less willing to recommend a candidate who outperforms them in their own area of strength.

## Why it is problematic

Recommendations and evaluations can be distorted without their author being aware of it.

## How to detect it

- A negative opinion comes from a direct competitor.
- Social comparisons are staged to encourage consumption.

## How to protect yourself

- Check whether there is an interest in making the comparison in this way.
- Ask for several independent opinions.
