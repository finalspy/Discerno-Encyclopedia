## How it works

Baron, Gowda and Kunreuther (1993) observe a preference for the total elimination of a risk, at the expense of a larger reduction in overall risk. Certainty has a value of its own that exceeds the calculated gain.

## Why it is problematic

A promise of "zero risk" is appealing, although it may direct resources away from the most serious dangers, or simply be unattainable.

## How to detect it

- Slogans such as "zero tolerance" or "without any risk".
- The overall residual risk is never quantified.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare the number of cases avoided by each option.
- Ask what remains, and at what cost.
