## How it works

Staw (1976) observes that participants personally responsible for a poor investment then devote more funds to it than those who are not responsible for it.

## Why it is problematic

Projects, conflicts and strategies can continue well beyond what is reasonable, because stopping would amount to admitting an error.

## How to detect it

- "We have gone too far to stop."
- New spending is justified by the earlier spending.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate what comes next as a new decision.
- Set stopping criteria in advance, and entrust the evaluation to someone else.
