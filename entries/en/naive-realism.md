## How it works

Robinson and colleagues (1995) observe that partisans of opposing camps overestimate the gap separating them and the extreme character of opposing positions.

## Why it is problematic

Disagreement becomes suspect, and the other's real positions are poorly understood. This fuels polarization.

## How to detect it

- "If you had the facts, you would agree."
- The opposing camp is described by its most extreme positions.

## How to protect yourself

- Restate the other's position until they recognize themselves in it.
- Look for surveys on what people actually think.
