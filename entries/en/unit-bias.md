## How it works

Geier, Rozin and Doros (2006) observe that changing the size of a portion or a utensil changes the amount consumed, because a unit is perceived as appropriate.

## Why it is problematic

The size of a package, a subscription or an episode can steer consumption without anyone actually deciding.

## How to detect it

- A portion is presented as a standard.
- The next episode, or the next dose, starts automatically.

## How to protect yourself

- Decide on the quantity before starting.
- Look at the actual content rather than the packaging.
