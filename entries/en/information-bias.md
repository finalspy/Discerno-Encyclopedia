## How it works

Baron, Beattie and Hershey (1988) observe that participants request tests that could not change the treatment. Information seems to have a value in itself.

## Why it is problematic

Accumulating data can delay a decision and give a false impression of control. Demands for "even more studies" can also serve to never decide.

## How to detect it

- Requests for data without saying what they would change.
- The decision is postponed although everything is available.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask: "What would I do differently depending on the answer?"
- Set a deadline for deciding.
