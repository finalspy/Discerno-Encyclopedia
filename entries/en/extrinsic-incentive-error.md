## How it works

Heath (1999) observes that people attribute to others more extrinsic motivations than they recognize in themselves, money for example, rather than the interest of the work.

## Why it is problematic

This bias leads people to explain others' positions by money or interest, without evidence, and to design incentives poorly.

## How to detect it

- "They only do it for the money", with no evidence.
- One describes oneself by one's values, and others by their gains.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask people what motivates them, rather than assuming it.
- Require evidence for an assumed interest.
