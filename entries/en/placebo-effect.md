## Definition

The word refers to the improvement observed after an inactive treatment. It may combine expectations, the natural course of the illness and regression to the mean.

## Competing definitions and debates

Hróbjartsson and Gøtzsche (2001) find little overall effect compared with no treatment, except on certain subjective outcomes such as pain. Benedetti (2008) describes real biological mechanisms in some situations.

## Why it helps to discern

It explains why a testimonial is not enough to establish the effectiveness of a treatment, and why blinded control groups are used.
