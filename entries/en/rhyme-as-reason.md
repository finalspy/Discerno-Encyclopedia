## How it works

McGlone and Tofighbakhsh (2000) present aphorisms that rhyme or are reworded without rhyme. Rhyming versions are judged more accurate. The ease of processing a sentence seems to be taken as a sign of truth.

## Why it is problematic

A well-turned slogan can give an appearance of wisdom to a claim with no foundation.

## How to detect it

- A striking formula replaces the explanation.
- The slogan loses its force when it is restated plainly.

## How to protect yourself

- Restate the sentence without rhyme and judge it again.
- Ask what supports it.
