## How it works

Mastroianni and Gilbert (2023) analyze surveys in many countries: people perceive a moral decline, whereas direct measures from one generation to the next do not show it. Some changes are genuinely negative, and this can be checked with data.

## Why it is problematic

The impression of decline can be exploited by any message that promises to restore a golden age, whatever it may be.

## How to detect it

- "Everything is getting worse and worse", with no numerical series.
- The reference past is never specified.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask which data compare the two eras.
- Examine each area separately.
