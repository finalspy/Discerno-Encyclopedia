## How it works

Johnson, Hashtroudi and Lindsay (1993) describe source attribution as a reconstruction made from cues, and prone to error. One may thus believe one read in a newspaper what one actually saw on a forum, or even imagined it.

## Why it is problematic

A rumor can be retained as serious information, and a doubtful source forgotten, while the content remains.

## How to detect it

- "I read somewhere that..."
- The source cannot be found.

## How to protect yourself

- Find the source before repeating a piece of information.
- Keep references when it matters.
