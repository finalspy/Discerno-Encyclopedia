## How it works

Parkinson (1958) imagines a committee that quickly approves a nuclear power plant, then debates the bicycle shed at length, a subject on which everyone feels competent. It is a satirical observation rather than an experimental finding.

Status: a satirical observation, not an established law.

## Why it is problematic

Public debate can focus on easy details while major choices pass without scrutiny.

## How to detect it

- Discussion time is inversely proportional to the stakes.
- A minor controversy overshadows a substantive issue.

## How to protect yourself

- Allocate time according to the stakes, in advance.
- Ask which important decision is not being discussed.
