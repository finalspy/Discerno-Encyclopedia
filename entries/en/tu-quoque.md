## How it works

Attention shifts from the argument to the consistency of its author. Walton (1998) classes this procedure among the forms of personal attack. An inconsistency can be a relevant fact about the person, but it does not show whether their criticism is correct.

## Why it is problematic

It is enough to find a fault in the other person to avoid the question, and the exchange can turn into mutual accusations.

## How to detect it

- "And you do the same."
- The initial criticism receives no answer.

## How to protect yourself

- Answer the substance first, then deal with the inconsistency separately.
- Acknowledge that an argument can be correct even when it comes from an inconsistent person.
