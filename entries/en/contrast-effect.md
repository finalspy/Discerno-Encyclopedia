## How it works

Kenrick and Gutierres (1980) show that the same person is judged less attractive after seeing a very attractive person. Mussweiler (2003) describes how these comparisons steer many judgments.

## Why it is problematic

The point of comparison can be chosen to flatter an offer, a figure or a track record.

## How to detect it

- A crossed-out price or an extreme case comes before the information.
- The comparison is given, but not the choice of it.

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate the option on its own, then compare it with several reference points.
- Ask why this reference point was chosen.
