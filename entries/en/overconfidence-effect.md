## How it works

Moore and Healy (2008) distinguish three phenomena that are often confused: overestimating one's performance, placing oneself too high relative to others, and being too certain of one's estimates.

## Why it is problematic

A confident assertion often persuades better than a more nuanced one, even though confidence does not measure accuracy.

## How to detect it

- No margin of error is given.
- Categorical predictions are never reassessed.

## How to protect yourself

- Give ranges and probabilities, then compare them with the results.
- Value the person who says "I don't know."
