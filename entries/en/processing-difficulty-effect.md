## How it works

O'Brien and Myers (1985) observe that additional effort to comprehend can improve memory for a text, because the reader elaborates on it more.

## Why it is problematic

Difficulty is not proof of depth. An obscure text can give the impression of a deserved discovery.

## How to detect it

- A complicated style gives an impression of seriousness.
- Having made an effort seems to prove that the content is true.

## How to protect yourself

- Restate in simple terms what one has understood.
- Ask whether the obscurity is necessary.
