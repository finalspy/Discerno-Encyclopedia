## How it works

MacDonald, Nail and Harper (2011) examine situations in which a person expresses the opposite of what they want in order to influence another. The technique relies on reactance: the refusal to let oneself be told how to behave.

## Why it is problematic

The person believes they made a free decision, when they are responding to a staged setup, sometimes even in advertisements.

## How to detect it

- "This product is surely not for you."
- A challenge is issued: "you won't dare."

## How to protect yourself

- Ask oneself what one would do if no one had said anything.
- Decide on what one wants, not against what one is told.
