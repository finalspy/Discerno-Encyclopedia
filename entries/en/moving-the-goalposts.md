## How it works

The standard of proof is not fixed in advance, or it changes after the fact. Each element provided is judged insufficient and a new condition appears.

## Why it is problematic

No evidence can then be enough, and the debate can never conclude.

## How to detect it

- "Fine, but you would also need..." after each answer.
- The initial criterion disappears from the discussion.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask at the outset what would change one's mind.
- Recall the accepted criterion before answering.
