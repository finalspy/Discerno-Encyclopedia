## How it works

Gilbert and colleagues (2000) show that participants, after having adapted psychologically to an experience, attribute this improved well-being to an external agent rather than to themselves.

## Why it is problematic

A method, a guide or fate may receive the credit for a change that was due to time or to the person themselves.

## How to detect it

- "It happened to me for a good reason", attributed to a third party.
- A method claims an improvement that would have occurred without it.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare with what happens without the method.
- Acknowledge the role of time and of one's own adaptation.
