## How it works

An idea is rejected or accepted because of where it comes from. The source can matter: Hahn and Oaksford (2007) show that taking its reliability into account can be rational. It is not enough, however, to settle the validity of an argument that can be checked.

## Why it is problematic

The reasoning spares one from examining the content, and sound ideas may be rejected for a bad reason.

## How to detect it

- "It comes from X, so it is false", with nothing else.
- The history of an idea is given in place of a refutation.

## How to protect yourself

- Examine the argument and its evidence first.
- Use the source to know what to verify, not to conclude.
