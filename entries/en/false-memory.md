## How it works

Roediger and McDermott (1995) lead participants to "remember" words that were not in a list. Loftus and Pickrell (1995) manage, with the help of relatives, to implant in some the memory of a fictitious childhood event. This does not mean that memories are generally false, nor that any given testimony is.

## Why it is problematic

Conviction does not guarantee accuracy, and an idea that is repeated or imagined can become a memory.

## How to detect it

- A detailed memory appears after repeated suggestions.
- The accounts change with each retelling.

## How to protect yourself

- Look for dated traces.
- Avoid leading questions with witnesses.
