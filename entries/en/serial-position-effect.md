## How it works

Murdock (1962) had participants learn lists of words: the first items (primacy) and the last items (recency) are the best recalled.

## Why it is problematic

Conditions slipped into the middle, the main argument placed first, and a memorable conclusion: the order of presentation influences what people retain.

## How to detect it

- Unfavorable information is placed in the middle.
- Only the headline and the last sentence are strong.

## How to protect yourself

- Reread the middle of the text.
- Ask for the full list, then reorder it yourself.
