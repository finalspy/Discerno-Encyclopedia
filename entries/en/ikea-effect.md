## How it works

Norton, Mochon and Ariely (2012) show that participants are willing to pay more for an object they assembled themselves, when the assembly was carried through to completion.

## Why it is problematic

One may overrate one's own ideas or achievements, and a "customizable" offer may seem more valuable than it is.

## How to detect it

- Attachment to a solution because one created it.
- A service involves the user in order to increase their engagement.

## How to protect yourself

- Have your work evaluated by others.
- Compare with what already exists.
