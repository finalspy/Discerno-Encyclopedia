## How it works

Godden and Baddeley (1975) had divers learn words on land or underwater: recall was better in the same environment. Tulving (1974) explains part of forgetting by the absence of the right cues.

## Why it is problematic

A memory depends on the context in which it is retrieved, and a question or an image can steer what comes back to mind.

## How to detect it

- An image or a sound triggers an association rather than an argument.
- A testimony depends on how the person was put back into the situation.

## How to protect yourself

- Check what you know through several routes.
- Be wary of what a staged setting brings back.
