## How it works

The meta-analysis by Meissner and Brigham (2001) finds this gap across many studies. Exposure to faces and experience with them are among the explanations discussed.

## Why it is problematic

This effect can lead to misidentification, notably in eyewitness testimony.

## How to detect it

- An identification rests on a witness of a different origin, with no other element.
- "They all look alike", said of a group.

## How to protect yourself

- Corroborate identification testimony.
- Be wary of your own certainty in these situations.
