## How it works

Skitka and her colleagues (1999) observe errors caused by following an automated aid, and others caused by failing to react when it signals nothing. Parasuraman and Manzey (2010) link this bias to the way attention slackens. It concerns any system, including analysis tools such as this one.

## Why it is problematic

A machine's output appears objective, although it depends on its data and its settings.

## How to detect it

- "The algorithm says so", with no further verification.
- An AI answer is cited as a source.

## How to protect yourself

- Check important results by another route.
- Ask what data and limits the system has.
