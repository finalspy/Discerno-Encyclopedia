## How it works

Shefrin and Statman (1985) describe this tendency, which would partly be explained by a refusal to realize a loss.

## Why it is problematic

It can reduce portfolio performance, and financial advice can exploit it: "wait, it will go back up".

## How to detect it

- A decision depends on the purchase price, not on the prospects.
- "I won't sell until I get my stake back."

## How to protect yourself

- Ask yourself whether you would buy this asset today at this price.
- Set written rules in advance.
