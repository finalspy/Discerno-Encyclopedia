## Definition

Allport (1954) describes it as an attitude based on rigid generalization, directed at a group or at a person because they belong to it.

## Competing definitions and debates

Definitions vary: some stress hostility, others the justification of inequalities, or the distinction between attitude and discrimination, which is an act. No group is exempt from it or uniquely targeted by it.

## Why it helps to discern

Distinguishing stereotype (belief), prejudice (attitude) and discrimination (act) helps to name precisely what one observes, and to avoid accusing on the basis of an impression.
