## How it works

The meta-analysis by Reed, Chan and Mikels (2014) confirms this age difference on average, with variations depending on study conditions.

## Why it is problematic

An average age says nothing about a specific person, and comparisons between generations are sometimes skewed by this memory mechanism.

## How to detect it

- A purely positive memory is used as a reference.
- A conclusion about an entire generation is drawn from an average trend.

## How to protect yourself

- Check the past with period data.
- Do not apply an average to an individual.
