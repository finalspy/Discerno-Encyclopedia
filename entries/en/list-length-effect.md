## How it works

Gillund and Shiffrin (1984) propose a model of memory that explains this effect in particular: items compete during recall.

## Why it is problematic

A long enumeration drowns its items, and certain conditions then go unnoticed.

## How to detect it

- A very long list of points or conditions.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask for the three essential elements.
- Read lists in writing rather than hearing them aloud.
