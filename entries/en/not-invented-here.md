## How it works

Katz and Allen (1982) study research and development teams. Over time, the most stable ones communicate less with the outside and obtain poorer results.

## Why it is problematic

Useful solutions are discarded or redone at great cost, simply because of their origin.

## How to detect it

- An outside idea is criticized before being tested.
- What already exists is redeveloped without a specific reason.

## How to protect yourself

- Compare the idea with what exists, on written criteria.
- Seek outside perspectives on a regular basis.
