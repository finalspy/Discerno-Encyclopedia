## How it works

Allport (1954) links stereotypes to our tendency to categorize. Devine (1989) distinguishes between knowledge of a stereotype, which is often automatic, and endorsement of it, which can be controlled. A statistical generalization says nothing about a specific individual.

## Why it is problematic

It can lead to unfair judgments and discrimination, and it serves as the basis for divisive rhetoric against any group at all.

## How to detect it

- "Like all X..."
- Membership in a group is given as the explanation for an individual act.

## How to protect yourself

- Find out about the person rather than their category.
- Check the generalization against data.
