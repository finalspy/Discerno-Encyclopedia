## Definition

Janis (1972) describes this mechanism from historical decision-making failures: illusion of invulnerability, self-censorship, pressure on those who doubt, and a facade of unanimity.

## Competing definitions and debates

Turner and Pratkanis (1998) review the research: the full model has received uneven support, and its strength rests in part on case studies.

## Why it helps to discern

The notion helps to spot warning signs in any group, including one's own: absence of dissent, stifled disagreements, risks left unexamined.
