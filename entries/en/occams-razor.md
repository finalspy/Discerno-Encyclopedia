## Definition

The principle proposes not to multiply hypotheses without necessity. It does not say that the simplest explanation is always true: it serves to choose between hypotheses that explain the facts equally well.

Status: a methodological principle, not a claim about the world.

## Competing definitions and debates

Sober (2015) shows that its justification depends on context: simplicity does not have the same meaning or the same value across problems.

## Why it helps to discern

The razor helps to set aside hypotheses that add unnecessary causes, but it should not be used to reject a complex explanation when the facts require it.
