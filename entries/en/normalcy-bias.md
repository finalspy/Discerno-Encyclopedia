## How it works

Research on disasters, summarized by Drabek (1986), shows that people often seek to confirm an alert and first interpret the signs as ordinary. Reaction time can be lengthened as a result.

## Why it is problematic

This reflex can delay an evacuation or a decision. Conversely, multiplying false alarms worsens it.

## How to detect it

- "That has never happened here."
- Warning signs are reinterpreted so as to remain reassuring.

## How to protect yourself

- Follow official instructions without waiting for additional evidence.
- Prepare in advance what one would do in case of an alert.
