## How it works

Schacter (1999) lists absent-mindedness among the ordinary failures of memory: the information was not processed with enough attention to be retained.

## Why it is problematic

Reading or signing while distracted can make us miss a clause, and continuous streams of information keep us in that state.

## How to detect it

- We accept terms without remembering having read them.

## How to protect yourself

- Make important decisions away from notifications.
- Check what you have agreed to.
