## How it works

Liu and colleagues (2014) observe that participants told that faces might be present see them in simple visual noise. The brain detects these patterns easily, and expectation strengthens the impression.

## Why it is problematic

A stain, a shadow or a noise can be presented as a sign or a hidden message.

## How to detect it

- A circle or a comment guides what to see in the image.
- The shape is no longer visible without the indication.

## How to protect yourself

- Look at the original, without annotation.
- Ask whether others would see the same thing without being primed for it.
