## How it works

Monin and Miller (2001) observe that participants who are given the chance to distance themselves from prejudice subsequently express certain preferences more freely. The size and robustness of the effect are debated.

## Why it is problematic

A good deed, a statement or a badge can serve as a justification. Organizations can also highlight a gesture to divert attention from the rest.

## How to detect it

- A virtuous gesture is recalled when justifying other conduct.
- "I cannot be X, since I did Y."

## How to protect yourself

- Evaluate each act on its own.
- Look at the full record of an organization, not its most visible action.
