## How it works

Gilovich, Vallone and Tversky (1985) conclude that streaks of successes in basketball do not exceed what chance predicts. Miller and Sanjurjo (2018) showed a statistical bias in that analysis, and their results support a real effect in some cases. The debate shows that one must distinguish excessive belief from absence of effect.

## Why it is problematic

A short run of success can make luck look like talent, in finance for example.

## How to detect it

- "He can't miss", after a short streak.
- A recent performance is extrapolated without any other evidence.

## How to protect yourself

- Look at results over a long period.
- Compare with what chance would produce.
