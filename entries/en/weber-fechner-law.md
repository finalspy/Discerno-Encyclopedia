## Definition

The same difference is noticed less as quantities grow: adding €10 to €20 is perceived more strongly than adding it to €2,000. Dehaene (2003) links this principle to our mental representation of numbers.

Status: an empirical law of psychophysics, which describes an approximate tendency.

## Competing definitions and debates

The law is an approximation, more or less accurate depending on the senses and the quantities involved.

## Why it helps to discern

It helps explain why an add-on seems small within a large purchase, and why very large numbers are hard to compare.
