## How it works

Edwards (1968) compares participants' judgments with a probability calculation. They move in the right direction, but not enough. The word does not refer to any political position here.

## Why it is problematic

A first impression resists evidence, and corrections have only a partial effect.

## How to detect it

- The position stays the same whatever the strength of the evidence.
- An initial estimate serves as a reference for a very long time.

## How to protect yourself

- Write in advance what a given piece of evidence should change.
- Re-evaluate from scratch from time to time.
