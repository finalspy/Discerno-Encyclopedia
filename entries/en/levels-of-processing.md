## How it works

Craik and Lockhart (1972) propose that the trace left depends on the depth of processing: thinking about the meaning of a word is more effective than looking at its form.

## Why it is problematic

Scrolling through headlines leaves few precise traces, but a general impression, which can steer judgment without being verified.

## How to detect it

- One believes one knows a subject because one has seen its headlines.

## How to protect yourself

- Read the full article on important subjects.
- Rephrase in your own words.
