## How it works

In Duncker's (1945) candle problem, the task is to fix a candle to a wall using a box of thumbtacks. Many people do not think of using the box as a support, because it is seen only as a container.

## Why it is problematic

The same block affects ideas: a problem presented in only one way seems to admit only one type of solution.

## How to detect it

- "It only serves that purpose."
- Only one category of solution is examined.

## How to protect yourself

- Describe an object or a problem without naming its function.
- Ask people from other fields.
