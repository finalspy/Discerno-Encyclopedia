## How it works

Pettigrew (1979) extends the attribution error to the level of groups. Hewstone (1990) reviews the studies and confirms a tendency, while qualifying its generality.

## Why it is problematic

The mechanism sustains stereotypes, because no action by the other group can then disprove them.

## How to detect it

- A fault by one's own side is an exception; a fault by the others is "typical".
- A success by the others is explained by luck or cheating.

## How to protect yourself

- Apply the reversal test to the proposed explanation.
- Look for causes of the same type for both groups.
