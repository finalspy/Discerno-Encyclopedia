## How it works

Repeating proves nothing, but it makes an idea easier to process. Fazio and colleagues (2015) show that repetition increases perceived truth, including for claims that participants could have recognized as false.

## Why it is problematic

The frequency of a message can give the impression of an established fact, and even a correction can repeat it.

## How to detect it

- The same formula returns without any new element.
- One has the feeling of "having already heard it", without a source.

## How to protect yourself

- Ask what has been added, apart from the repetition.
- Check a familiar claim as if it were new.
