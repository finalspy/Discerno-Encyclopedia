## Definition

Miller (1956) observes limits at about seven items. He notes that these items can be grouped into larger units.

## Competing definitions and debates

Cowan (2001) argues for an estimate closer to four units. The idea of a universal rule for presentations is often a simplification.

## Why it helps to discern

It helps to spot when a flow of arguments exceeds what one can follow, and to ask for the essentials.
