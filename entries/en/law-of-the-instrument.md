## Definition

The philosopher Abraham Kaplan (Kaplan, 1964) states it about research methods: problems are formulated so that the techniques one has mastered can answer them. The psychologist Abraham Maslow (Maslow, 1966) gives the most quoted version: if all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail.

Status: an observation about practices, not a measured law.

## Competing definitions and debates

It concerns researchers as well as experts, institutions and each of us: a single analytical grid, even one that is right in its own field, distorts whatever falls outside that field.

## Why it helps to discern

When one explanation comes back for every problem, ask whether it comes from the facts or from the tool of the person putting it forward. This also applies to this encyclopedia: not every disagreement is a bias or a manipulation.
