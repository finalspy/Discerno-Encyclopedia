## Definition

Cohen (1972) describes how media, officials and the public amplify a phenomenon and identify culprits. Goode and Ben-Yehuda (1994) propose criteria: concern, hostility, consensus, disproportion and volatility.

## Competing definitions and debates

The criterion of disproportion is the most debated: who decides that a concern is excessive? The term itself can be used to discredit a well-founded concern.

## Why it helps to discern

The notion invites comparing the concern with the data, without presuming the outcome. The reversal test applies, whichever camp is raising the alarm.
