## How it works

Zwicky (2005) named this effect about language usage. Many uses criticized as new are found in older texts. The phenomenon is close to the frequency illusion.

## Why it is problematic

One may wrongly conclude that a language, a generation or a society is declining.

## How to detect it

- "In the past, people did not say that", without historical verification.
- Novelty is inferred from one's own personal discovery of it.

## How to protect yourself

- Consult historical dictionaries and corpora.
- Look for a date before speaking of novelty.
