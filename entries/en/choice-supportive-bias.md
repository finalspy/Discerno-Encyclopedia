## How it works

Brehm (1956) observes that after a choice between two objects, the chosen object is rated more favorably and the other less favorably. The process eases the discomfort of giving something up.

## Why it is problematic

An error becomes hard to recognize, and one defends one's choice instead of evaluating it. Reassuring messages after a purchase rely on this.

## How to detect it

- One looks mainly for opinions that confirm what was chosen.
- The flaws of the option selected are minimized.

## How to protect yourself

- Before deciding, write down what will allow the outcome to be judged.
- Ask someone who has no stake in the choice for an opinion.
